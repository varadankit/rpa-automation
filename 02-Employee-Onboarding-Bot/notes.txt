Project Name: Employee Onboarding Automation Bot

Objective:
Automate repetitive HR onboarding tasks such as folder creation, email generation, and onboarding checklist preparation.

Problem Statement:
HR teams manually perform onboarding tasks for each new employee, which leads to inefficiency and inconsistency.

Inputs:
- Employee details in Excel file:
  - Name
  - Email
  - Department
  - Role

Expected Outputs:
- Created folder structure for employee
- Welcome email draft or sent email
- Onboarding checklist document
- Entry updated in tracking sheet

Key Automation Steps:
1. Read employee data from Excel
2. Create employee folder structure
3. Generate onboarding checklist
4. Draft/send welcome email
5. Update onboarding status in Excel

Business Value:
- Reduces HR workload
- Ensures consistent onboarding process
- Improves employee experience

Exception Handling:
- Missing employee data
- Folder already exists
- Email sending failure
