Project Name: Support Ticket Classification Automation Bot

Objective:
Automatically classify incoming support emails/tickets into categories and store structured data for reporting.

Problem Statement:
Support teams receive large volumes of emails and manually categorize them, leading to delays in response and inefficiency.

Inputs:
- Email subject and body content
- Support inbox or Excel dataset of tickets

Expected Outputs:
- Categorized ticket data in Excel:
  - Ticket ID
  - Category (Password, Hardware, Software, Access, Other)
  - Priority (optional)
- Log of processed tickets

Key Automation Steps:
1. Read incoming emails or ticket data
2. Extract subject and body text
3. Apply keyword-based classification logic
4. Assign category to each ticket
5. Store structured output in Excel
6. Generate summary report

Business Value:
- Faster ticket routing
- Improved support response time
- Reduced manual classification workload

Exception Handling:
- Empty or unreadable emails
- Unclassified tickets
- Duplicate entries
